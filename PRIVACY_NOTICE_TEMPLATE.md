## Clarius RAW Data Downloader

**Operator:** [insert responsible institution/research group]

**Privacy contact:** [insert approved contact and email]

The application connects to Clarius Cloud using credentials entered by the
authorized operator. The password is used only for the current application run,
is not written to the application's settings file, and is cleared from the
application after the run. The operator's email address and non-sensitive app
preferences may be stored locally on that Windows account.

The application retrieves exam and capture metadata and downloads available RAW
archives from Clarius Cloud to a folder chosen by the operator. Downloaded study
data, capture identifiers, synchronization timestamps, and operational logs are
stored in that chosen location. The application does not transmit analytics,
advertising identifiers, or telemetry to the app developer.

The organization operating Clarius Cloud and any approved storage provider may
process data under their own agreements and privacy practices. Operators must
use only authorized accounts and institutionally approved storage locations.

For access, correction, deletion, retention, security-incident, or other privacy
questions concerning research data processed with this application, contact the
responsible institution using the contact above.
